# SafeArrive 🛡️

> Arrive Safe. Stay Connected.

SafeArrive is a free, open-source Flutter app that automatically sends 
a WhatsApp message to your family or friends the moment you reach 
your destination — no manual texting needed.

Built for students, working women, and solo travelers worldwide.

---

## The Problem

Every day, millions of people — especially students living in hostels 
and women traveling alone — have to manually text their families 
to say they arrived safely. It is easy to forget. It causes anxiety. 
SafeArrive solves this automatically.

---

## Features

- 📍 **Auto GPS Detection** — detects when you reach your destination
- 💬 **Auto WhatsApp Message** — sends your custom message automatically
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Multiple Contacts** — notify family and friends at once
- ⏰ **Delay Alerts** — sends a warning if you have not arrived on time
- 🗺️ **Journey History** — view all your past trips and message status
- 🌍 **Multilingual** — supports English, Urdu, and Arabic
- 🔒 **Privacy First** — all data stored locally on your device

---

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Flutter (Dart)
- **Location:** Geolocator, Google Maps Flutter
- **Messaging:** WhatsApp via url_launcher
- **Storage:** shared_preferences (local, no server)
- **State Management:** Provider / Riverpod
- **UI:** Material Design 3, Google Fonts (Poppins)
